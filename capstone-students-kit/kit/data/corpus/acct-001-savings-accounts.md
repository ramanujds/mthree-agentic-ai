# Savings Accounts

## Everyday Saver
The Everyday Saver requires a minimum opening balance of $500. The monthly maintenance fee is $5. The fee is waived if the average monthly balance is at least $2,500, or if the account holder is under 25 years old. The account pays 1.2% APY.

## Premier Saver
The Premier Saver requires a minimum balance of $10,000 and has no monthly maintenance fee. It pays 3.1% APY. Premier Saver holders receive 5 free domestic wires per month; additional wires are charged at the rate in the fee schedule (FEES-001).

## Withdrawals
Savings accounts allow up to 6 withdrawals per month. Each additional withdrawal is charged the excess withdrawal fee in the fee schedule (FEES-001).
