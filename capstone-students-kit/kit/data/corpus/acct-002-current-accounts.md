# Current Accounts

## Basic Current
The Basic Current account has a monthly fee of $8, waived if monthly deposits are at least $1,500. An overdraft facility of up to $500 is available; overdrawn items are charged the overdraft fee in the fee schedule (FEES-001). A debit card is included.

## Business Current
The Business Current account has a monthly fee of $25 and includes 20 free domestic wires per month. Additional domestic wires are charged at the rate in the fee schedule (FEES-001). Business accounts require a beneficial ownership declaration (see KYC-001).

## Frozen and closed accounts
A frozen account blocks all debits; credits are still permitted. Accounts are frozen during a KYC review or on a court order. A closed account cannot be reopened; the customer must open a new account.
