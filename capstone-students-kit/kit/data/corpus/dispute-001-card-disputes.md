# Card Disputes

## Reporting window
Customers must report a disputed transaction within 60 days of the statement date.

## Provisional credit
Provisional credit is applied within 10 business days of a valid dispute being logged.

## Investigation
Investigations are completed within 45 days. Disputes involving international transactions may take up to 90 days. Disputes involving suspected fraud are referred to the Head of Compliance.
