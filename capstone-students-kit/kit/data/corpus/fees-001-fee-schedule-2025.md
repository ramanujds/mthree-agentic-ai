# Fee Schedule (effective 1 January 2025)

## Payments
- Outgoing domestic wire: $15 per wire
- Outgoing international wire: $40 per wire
- Incoming wire: $10 per wire
- Wire recall: $35 per request
- Stop payment: $20 per request

## Accounts and cards
- Overdraft fee: $25 per item, maximum 3 items per day
- Paper statement: $4 per month
- Expedited card replacement: $30
- Excess savings withdrawal: $10 per withdrawal beyond 6 per month
