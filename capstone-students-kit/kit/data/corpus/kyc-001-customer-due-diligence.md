# Customer Due Diligence

## Required documents
New customers must provide a government-issued photo ID and proof of address dated within the last 90 days.

## Enhanced due diligence
Politically exposed persons (PEPs) and customers linked to high-risk countries require enhanced due diligence, approved by the Head of Compliance.

## Retention
KYC records and audit logs are retained for 7 years after account closure.

## Re-verification
Customer details are re-verified every 3 years for standard-risk customers and every year for high-risk customers.
