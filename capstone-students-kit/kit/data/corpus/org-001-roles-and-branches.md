# Organisation, Roles and Branches

## North Region
Tomas Weber is the Regional Director of the North Region. The North Region is part of Meridian Bank. Two branches belong to the North Region: Harbor Street and Elm Park. Priya Raman is the Branch Manager of Harbor Street. Daniel Okafor is the Branch Manager of Elm Park.

## South Region
Lena Hoffmann is the Regional Director of the South Region. The South Region is part of Meridian Bank. One branch belongs to the South Region: Riverside. Marcus Chen is the Branch Manager of Riverside.

## Head Office
Ingrid Solberg is the Head of Payments at Meridian Bank. Rahul Mehta is the Head of Compliance and the Money Laundering Reporting Officer at Meridian Bank. Aisha Bello is the Head of Customer Support at Meridian Bank.
