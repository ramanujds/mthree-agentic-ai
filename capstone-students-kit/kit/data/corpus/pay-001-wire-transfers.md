# Wire Transfer Policy

## Cut-off times
Domestic wires must be submitted by 4:00 PM local time. International wires must be submitted by 1:00 PM local time. Wires submitted after the cut-off are processed on the next business day.

## Limits
Online wires are limited to $25,000 per customer per day. Branch-initiated wires are not subject to the online limit but are subject to the approval matrix (RISK-001).

## Dual control
Wires of $10,000 or more require dual control: two different approvers must approve the wire. An initiator can never approve their own wire.

## New beneficiaries
An online-initiated wire to a new beneficiary is held for 24 hours before release. Any AI-initiated payment to a new beneficiary also needs human approval (see SEC-001).

## Recalls
A wire recall request must be raised within 5 business days of the wire date.
