# Payment Approval Matrix

## Single-approver limits
The approval authority needed depends on the wire amount:
- Teller: up to $5,000
- Branch Manager: up to $25,000
- Regional Director: up to $100,000
- Head of Payments: any amount above $100,000

## Rules
The final approver must hold authority for the full amount of the wire. Where dual control applies (PAY-001), the second approver must be a different person. If the required approver is unavailable, the request escalates one level up. Written delegation is allowed for a maximum of 5 business days.
