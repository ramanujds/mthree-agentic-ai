# Support Escalation and Priorities

## Escalation path
Issues move from Tier 1 to Tier 2 to the Duty Manager. Every escalation ticket must include the account id, a reason and a priority.

## Priorities
- P1 (service outage or suspected fraud): first response within 15 minutes
- P2 (failed or delayed money movement): first response within 2 hours
- P3 (general enquiries): first response within 1 business day

## Fraud and complaints
Suspected fraud must be reported to the Head of Compliance within 24 hours. Customer complaints are owned by the Head of Customer Support.
