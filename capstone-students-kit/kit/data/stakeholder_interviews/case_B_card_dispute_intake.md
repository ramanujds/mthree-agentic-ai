# Interview transcript - Case B: Card dispute intake
Interviewee: Marcus Chen, Branch Manager Riverside (acting as dispute operations lead). Interviewer: you. (Synthetic case.)

**You:** How do disputes start?
**Marcus:** A customer calls or walks in and says a transaction is wrong. A colleague fills in a paper form, then re-types it into the dispute system. About half the forms have missing fields: no merchant name, wrong date, no statement date.
**You:** What are the rules?
**Marcus:** Customer has 60 days from the statement date. We must give provisional credit within 10 business days. Investigation within 45 days, 90 for international.
**You:** Where do cases get stuck?
**Marcus:** Missing information, mostly. And international ones, nobody remembers they have a longer clock. Sometimes we credit twice by accident because two colleagues log the same dispute.
**You:** What do you want from automation?
**Marcus:** Check the form is complete, spot duplicates, work out the deadline, and draft the provisional credit. I want it to just do the credit, to save time.
**You:** Is there a limit on credits a colleague can approve?
**Marcus:** Over $5,000 needs the regional director. I think. Ask Ingrid, it might have changed.
**You:** How many disputes?
**Marcus:** Around 400 a month, maybe 40 of them international. Fraud-related ones are about 30 a month and those go to Compliance.
**You:** How would you measure success?
**Marcus:** Fewer incomplete forms, no duplicate credits, nobody missing a deadline.
