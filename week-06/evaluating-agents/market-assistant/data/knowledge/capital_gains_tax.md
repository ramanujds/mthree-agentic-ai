# Capital Gains Tax on Listed Equity Shares

## Short-term and long-term
Gains from selling listed equity shares are taxed as capital gains. The holding period decides the type. If the shares are held for 12 months or less before selling, the gain is a short-term capital gain (STCG). If the shares are held for more than 12 months, the gain is a long-term capital gain (LTCG). The holding period is counted from the buy date to the sell date.

## Tax rates
STCG on listed equity shares is taxed at a flat 20%. LTCG on listed equity shares is taxed at 12.5%, but only on the total long-term gains above Rs. 1,25,000 in a financial year. Long-term gains up to Rs. 1,25,000 in a financial year are exempt from tax.

## How the gain is computed
Capital gain = (sell price - buy price) x quantity. Tax is applied only on a positive gain. Short-term losses can be set off against both short-term and long-term gains. Long-term losses can be set off only against long-term gains.

## Cess
A 4% health and education cess on the tax amount exists in real returns but is ignored by this assistant.
