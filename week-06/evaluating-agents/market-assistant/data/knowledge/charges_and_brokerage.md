# Charges and Brokerage

## Brokerage
Brokerage for delivery and intraday equity orders is Rs. 20 or 0.03% of the trade value, whichever is lower, per executed order. Example: for a trade value of Rs. 38,500, 0.03% is Rs. 11.55, which is lower than Rs. 20, so brokerage is Rs. 11.55. For a trade value of Rs. 1,00,000, 0.03% is Rs. 30, so the Rs. 20 cap applies and brokerage is Rs. 20.

## Securities Transaction Tax (STT)
STT is charged on delivery trades at 0.1% of the trade value on both the buy side and the sell side. STT is collected by the government through the broker.

## GST on brokerage
Goods and Services Tax (GST) of 18% is charged on the brokerage amount only, not on the trade value.

## Total charges per order
Total charges for one executed order = brokerage + STT + GST on brokerage. For a buy order, the cash debited is the trade value plus total charges. For a sell order, the cash credited is the trade value minus total charges.

## Charges ignored by this assistant
Exchange transaction charges, SEBI turnover fees and stamp duty exist in real accounts but are ignored by this assistant to keep the numbers simple.
