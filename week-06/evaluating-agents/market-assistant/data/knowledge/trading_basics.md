# Trading Basics (NSE and BSE)

## Exchanges and indices
India has two main stock exchanges: the National Stock Exchange (NSE) and the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE). The Nifty 50 is the benchmark index of NSE and tracks 50 large companies. The Sensex is the benchmark index of BSE and tracks 30 large companies. This assistant trades NSE-listed equity shares only.

## Trading hours
The NSE equity market is open Monday to Friday, from 9:15 AM to 3:30 PM IST. The pre-open session runs from 9:00 AM to 9:15 AM. The market is closed on Saturdays, Sundays and exchange holidays. Orders cannot be executed while the market is closed.

## Settlement cycle
Indian equity markets follow a T+1 settlement cycle. A trade made on day T is settled on the next working day: the buyer receives the shares in the demat account and the seller receives the money. In this paper-trading sandbox, sale proceeds are credited to the available cash balance immediately.

## Demat and trading accounts
A trading account is used to place buy and sell orders. A demat account holds the shares in electronic form. Both are needed to invest in shares, and both require KYC (identity and address proof, and a PAN card). Demat accounts can have a nominee.

## SEBI
The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) is the regulator of the securities market. SEBI protects investors, regulates brokers and exchanges, and frames rules against insider trading and market manipulation.
